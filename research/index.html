Reading Labor-Market Pressure Through Buffer Sectors: Evidence from Guangzhou Ride-Hailing.

Using monthly data from Guangzhou’s ride-hailing market, I study when changes in driver activity and receipts can reveal weaker job opportunities elsewhere. The challenge is to distinguish people turning to driving because other jobs pay less or are harder to find from people driving more because driving itself has become a better deal.

Decomposing Urban Housing Values in China: An Intergenerational Migration Hedge Model.

Why do housing prices in major Chinese cities remain high relative to rents? This paper argues that homeownership can insure children’s future access to urban employment under housing-linked hukou restrictions. A two-period model shows how this intergenerational hedge contributes to housing values: under the baseline calibration, removing these restrictions lowers households’ housing reservation value by 37.5%.
