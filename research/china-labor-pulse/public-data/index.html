What public data can identify

Prototype · Guangzhou ride-hailing · 14 Nov 2026

Background

Ride-hailing is treated as a buffer. Reading it as outside employment still needs labour input and the driver’s net return, not just the published counts.

L = N × h R = z − d

N daily active drivers. h hours per active driver-day. z gross receipts. d deductions. Monthly reports give N and z. They do not give h or d, and they do not define “active.”

Question

Can public data pin down net return and labour input tightly enough to read March–June 2026 as external labour-market pressure?

Net return

  • 2023 rules require a commission ceiling and a per-order display. That is not a citywide average.
  • 18.8% median (18–20% for most drivers) is a multi-province survey, restated in a blue-book mirror. A level reference only.
  • The rental template and 28 local firms show the contract form. No rents, no adoption rate.
  • Court mirrors show rent taken from receipts before withdrawal (CNY 4,200/month, 900/week, 3,900/month). Too few, and dispute-selected, to be a price index.
  • National IPH is about CNY 27 in 2024, after commission. A six-city rental band including Guangzhou is CNY 3,000–4,000. Neither converts Guangzhou z into net return.

Labour input

  • 2024 survey: 28% at most 8 hours, 63% at 9–12, 9% above 12. For 2025: 60% at 9–12, 10% above 12. Bins, not a March–June mean.
  • A 2024 “salary” about CNY 6,000 above 2023 is not defined as post-cost income, and does not contradict this window.
  • A 2020 sample: 6.3 days/week, 10.7 hours/day. Too old to rescale 2026.
  • Dongguan hours (9.7) cover vehicles with at least 10 orders. A national survey reports 6.41 hours. Neither applies to Guangzhou’s count.

Result

Inconclusive. The objects exist in the wider system. They are not identified here as a monthly series.

The result would change if the same city and platform universe published, each month:

  • hours per active driver-day, and the definition of active;
  • realised payout after commission, fees, and bonuses;
  • rent, energy, and other major costs, by ownership or rental type.
Public material through 14 Nov 2026. Does not update the prototype. Secondary mirrors are not administrative tables.
