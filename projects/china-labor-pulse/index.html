Project：China Informal Employment Pressure Index

Code · 22 Sep 2026

Developing a monthly measure of labor-market pressure in China, using crowding in food-delivery and ride-hailing markets as a signal of employment stress beyond the official unemployment rate.

  1. Reading
    —
  2. Frequency
    Monthly
  3. Coverage
    National. Cities later.
  4. Series
    Ride-hailing: driver permits, vehicle permits, orders. Food delivery: rider postings and the hourly pay offered. Orders and spending are held out as demand.
  5. Construction
    Crowding after demand is removed. The raw measure is orders per driver.

    Not posted.
