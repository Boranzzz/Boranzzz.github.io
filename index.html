Toward computational macro.

I’m a senior undergraduate at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, interested in macro finance and computational economics. I prefer work that has to answer to data — where a model’s claims can be taken apart, rebuilt, and tried again.

My current focus is building fluency in dynamic models, numerical methods, and the empirical tools used to take them to data. Longer term, I want to work as a computational economist on problems where markets, policy, and computation meet.
